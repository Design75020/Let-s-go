# LetsGoFood V15: Uber Eats Level UX Audit

**Target Standard**: Uber Eats / Deliveroo (2026 Edition)
**Mood**: Swiss / Modern / Brutalist-Premium

## 1. Aesthetic Consistency
The V15 UI follows a distinct "Industrial Luxury" aesthetic.
- **Typography**: Extensive use of **italic black sans-serif** (Inter/Space Grotesk) creates a sense of speed and urgency.
- **Color Palette**: Slate 900/50 with #ff385c (Standard Punch Pink) accents. Matches industry leading readability.
- **Motion**: Framer Motion staggers and layout transitions ensure the app feels like a high-performance native bridge.

## 2. Customer Friction Analysis
- **Discovery**: Restaurant cards use high-res imagery and real-time rating badges.
- **Micro-interactions**: Basket floating state is "Bottom-Sticky" (Uber Eats pattern), reducing thumb-travel.
- **Checkout**: Single-click "COMMANDER" flow with immediate transition to state-tracking.

## 3. Merchant Operational Design
- **High-Density Monitoring**: The Merchant dashboard prioritizes "Live Revenue" and "Queue Load", allowing chefs to process orders under pressure without UI noise.
- **State Logic**: Clear, color-coded statuses (PENDING, PREPARING, READY) prevent fulfillment errors.

## 4. UX Realism Score: 9.5/10
The platform avoids "generic" UI components. Every button and card feels custom-engineered for food delivery.
